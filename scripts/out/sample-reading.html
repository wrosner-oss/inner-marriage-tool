Right relationship

In Tantra, the thing to look for is always if your masculine side is serving your feminine side so she can fully blossom. Also to make sure she has a lot of space for expression as she is primary!

The idea is that the more space the feminine has to express herself, the happier we are! More life force energy. More radiance. More ability to manifest what we want.

Everything tends to go better when our inner masculine serves our inner feminine.

Some questions to sit with

  • Which of these aspects of you have gotten the most attention in the past? Does your feminine have what she needs?
  • What would it look like for your Taurus side to support and serve your Cancer side with total devotion?
  • Is she getting enough time questing for nurturing what she loves, tending what is growing, and creating a warm home?
  • Is your masculine using his skills (savoring the senses, beauty and pleasure, and working with his hands, for example) to provide a situation and life where she can thrive?
  • Is he using his building something solid and presence and patience with her (and not just with everyone else) in ways that serve her and give her the emotional closeness and caring for her people she desires?
  • Is your Pleasure Lover serving your Woman devoted to her clan/her people?
