# Inner Marriage Email — Reusable Text Library

Source: sent emails with subject "Your Inner Marriage according to Shamanic Astrology" (and its variants), pulled from Gmail. This is a reference of the copy blocks you reuse across readings, organized so you can assemble a new email by picking: **opening → Venus sign block → Mars sign block → right-relationship boilerplate → reflection questions → closing.**

Two voices show up across the archive:
- **Classic voice** — the long-running format ("ARIES - your feminine"), used in the large majority (~180+) of emails since 2023.
- **2026 voice** — a newer, more polished/poetic format ("✧ ARIES — Your Feminine") introduced March 2026, used for a smaller recent batch. Shorter, more line-broken, ✧/☀︎ bullet style.

Both are included per sign where found. Pick whichever voice matches the email you're writing, or mix (e.g. classic paragraph + 2026 bullet summary).

## Reusable structural blocks (not sign-specific)

### Opening — classic voice
> Dear [Name],
>
> Below is your special flavor of the masculine and feminine based on your birth chart.
>
> This isn't your whole birth chart. It's just the dynamic of your inner marriage. But the inner marriage is [hugely/really] important as it reflects in all areas of our life, particularly in our relationships and how we put our work out into the world. It also speaks to what makes us feel sweet and happy and in alignment.

### Opening — 2026 voice
> Dear [Name],
>
> I'm so happy to share this with you ☀︎
>
> Below is your *special flavor* of the masculine and feminine as reflected in your birth chart.
>
> This isn't your whole chart—just the dynamic of your inner marriage.
> But as you know, this piece touches so much…
> your relationships, your work, your sense of alignment…
> even what helps you feel deeply *at home* in yourself.

### "Small note" — Sun sign as fuel (template, both voices)
> Small note: in Shamanic Astrology, the Sun Sign isn't [so important / the most important piece]. It's [just / more like] the fuel you need to shine bright[, and feel like yourself]. As your Sun is in **[SIGN]**, that means you need all that [SIGN] goodness — [2-3 keywords for that sign] — to feel your best.

Use this line as a hook: if their Venus or Mars *also* happens to be in their Sun sign, call that out explicitly ("That said, your Mars/Venus is also in [Sign]. So this part of you should not be denied!") — this shows up often and personalizes the reading.

### Section header (classic voice)
> **Here below is your special flavor of your inner marriage based on your birth chart:**
>
> Your "feminine" side (represented by Venus) is in [SIGN]
> Your "masculine" side (represented by Mars) is in [SIGN]
>
> Here's the flavor and attitude of each:

### Section header (2026 voice)
> ## ☀︎ Your Inner Marriage
> Your feminine (Venus) is in **[SIGN]**
> Your masculine (Mars) is in **[SIGN]**
>
> [One line naming the pairing's theme, e.g. "This is a beautiful pairing of sensuality and radiance—a feminine that loves to savor life, supported by a masculine that is here to shine and lead."]

### Right Relationship (Tantra) — the core recurring paragraph, nearly verbatim in almost every email
> In Tantra, the thing to look for is always if your masculine side is serving your feminine side so she can fully blossom. Also to make sure she has a lot of space for expression as she is primary!
>
> The idea is that the more space the feminine has to express herself, the happier we are! More life force energy. More radiance. More ability to manifest what we want.
>
> Everything tends to go better when our inner masculine serves our inner feminine.

Add-on **if recipient is male-bodied**: "That said, as a man, both are hugely important and need honoring and full expression." / "AND both of these parts of you are incredibly important and need space to express and thrive."

2026-voice version:
> ✧ Right Relationship
>
> In Tantra, we're always looking for this: Is the masculine serving the feminine so she can fully blossom? Because the feminine is primary. The more space she has to express… the more life force, radiance, and creative power flows through your life. And everything tends to move more beautifully when the masculine is in devoted service to her.

### Reflection questions — generic template (fill in sign names/archetypes)
> Given that, some questions to reflect on might be:
>
> Do you relate to one of these — [Venus sign] or [Mars sign] — more than the other? Who has gotten the most attention/airtime during your life?
>
> Is your masculine using "his" skills ([Mars sign trait], for example) for "her" to [Venus sign desire/need]?
>
> Is your inner [Mars archetype] serving your inner [Venus archetype]?
>
> (And whatever else comes to you about their relationship!)

### Closing
> Much love, / Lots of love, / With love,
> Amelia [☀︎]
>
> *(signature block: Amelia Sunyata Perkins, mobile, Counseling / Workshops / Love experiment links)*

## Sign-by-sign text blocks

Each sign's **identity paragraph** (the quoted "I am ___" block) is used **verbatim whether the sign is playing Venus or Mars** — only the framing line ("your feminine" vs "your masculine") and the archetype list change. That identity paragraph is the most reusable atomic unit.

### ARIES (fire, 1st gate — individuation)
> "I am Aries, a fire sign. I create the first gate of awareness, through which I teach the meaning of individuation. I am active, high spirited, and energetic. I love excitement, adventure, play, and competition. As a sacred warrior, I will fight to defend my belief and protect what I value in life.
>
> I like to go first, birth the new, get something started, and lead the way. I am courageous, trusting, decisive, inspired and spontaneous."

Descriptive line: *Aries is a risk taker, likes adrenaline...*

- **Feminine archetypes:** Wild Woman Archetype, "Women Who Run with the Wolves," Warrior Amazon, Fighting for a Cause, The Tom Boy, Joan of Arc
- **Masculine archetypes (2026 voice):** The Warrior / Competitor, The Rugged Individualist, The Initiator, The Playful Risk-Taker

2026-voice descriptive: *Aries energy is a risk-taker. It loves adrenaline, movement, newness. It follows instinct. It says yes before everything is figured out. It is the force that starts things.*

Archetypes: Aphrodite, The Lover, The Artist, The Muse.

## Notes on assembly
- The **identity paragraph** + **descriptive line** + **archetype list** together form one "sign block" — same content whether it's playing Venus (feminine) or Mars (masculine); only the framing sentence and archetype list flip.
- The **Right Relationship** and **closing** sections barely vary — safe to copy-paste as-is each time.
- **Reflection questions** are the most bespoke part — they reference the specific archetype pairing (e.g. "Is your inner Husband/Peacemaker serving your inner Sorceress Witch Tantrica?"), so they need light editing per combination even when reusing sentence structure.
- Double (same-sign Venus+Mars) pairings get their own framing ("Yes — a double Leo," "They share the same fire") — worth keeping a couple of these one-liners handy for when Venus = Mars.
